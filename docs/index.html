clearview

A media player for your TV

Sign in with your own service account and browse live TV, the guide, movies and series in a clean, remote-friendly layout. Made for Fire TV and Android TV, and works on Android phones too.

Install on Fire TV

  1. Open Settings → My Fire TV → About and click the Fire TV Stick tile 7 times to unlock Developer options.
  2. In Developer options, allow Install unknown apps for Downloader.
  3. Install Downloader (orange icon) from the Fire TV app store.
  4. Open Downloader, type the link above, press Go, then Install and Open.

Install on an Android phone or tablet

  1. Open this page on the device and tap Download the APK.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.

Android may show a warning because the app isn’t from the Play Store. That’s normal for apps installed this way.

Good to know

  1. Clearview is only a player. It doesn’t include any channels, playlists or subscriptions. You need your own account details (server address, username and password) from your service provider. Works with Xtream Codes compatible accounts.
  2. Only use it with content you have the right to watch.
  3. Your details are stored only on your device and sent only to your provider’s server.
  4. You can switch background colours and choose VLC as the video player in Settings.
